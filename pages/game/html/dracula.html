Character Profile

Dracula Lord of the Vampires

Dracula in Unmatched is a relentless predator who drains life, manipulates positioning, and overwhelms opponents through attrition. He fights best at close range, feeding on enemy heroes and sidekicks to sustain himself.

Thematically, Dracula represents a strategic, calculated villain: he rarely wins by brute force alone. Instead, he:

  • Weakens enemies over time through life drain and chip damage.
  • Uses his Sisters to set up traps and flanks.
  • Rewards patient, opportunistic play and punishes careless advances.
