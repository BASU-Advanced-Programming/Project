How To Play

1. Select Your Hero

Remeber if you are younger you can choose your hero first :)

2. Place Your Hero

After hero selection you have to place you'r hero in one of the two positon shown below

3. Create your hand

Draw 5 cards from the top of your deck.

4. Take Actions

On your turn you must take 2 actions. You may choose the same action twice or two different ones.

Select an action above to see how it works.

5. Defeat Your Opponent

Reduce the enemy hero's health to zero to win the match.
